## Why

CSL-Daily is now the practical control dataset for rebuilding the synthetic camera-pose to mmWave-pose
pipeline and for checking the historical WaveLLM training direction.  The current system has a validated
synthetic engineering vertical slice, while the in-progress CSL-Daily simulation change rebuilds intake,
annotation, and cube materialization.  It does not yet define three controls required for a defensible
CSL-Daily reproduction matrix:

1. a baseline-versus-improved camera-pose annotation protocol and quality gate;
2. an explicit pose-only WaveLLM mode rather than a fusion model supplied with fabricated radar features; and
3. a checksum-bound OmniHand feature export plus cross-fitted predicted-pose inputs for the full
   predicted-mmWave-pose WaveLLM path.

Without these controls, a camera-pose semantic ceiling could be confused with a radar-fusion result, and
training WaveLLM on CubeNet predictions made by a model that trained on the same rows would create an
unmeasured train/inference mismatch.  They also separate a new synthetic CSL-Daily control from the paper's
real-radar and MANO/ray-tracing claims.

## What Changes

- Define a versioned CSL-Daily reproduction matrix with named `cam_pose`, `predicted_mmw_pose`, and
  `predicted_mmw_pose_plus_cube_feature` inputs.
- Receipt and classify any uploaded historical `sentence/poses`, synthetic signals, features, and legacy split
  mappings before regenerating them. A receipt-bound historical pose/signal path supports a labelled direct
  replay; it is preserved beside, not overwritten by, canonical annotation/simulation outputs.
- Add a first-class WaveLLM modality configuration.  `pose_only` accepts no radar feature, has no unused
  radar projector/fusion parameters, and is not implemented by supplying zeros.  `pose_plus_radar_feature`
  retains the existing fusion contract.
- Add a checksum-bound OmniHand frame-feature export contract.  Exported features bind a specific checkpoint,
  source manifest, split assignment, model fingerprint, frame mask, and temporal frame identity.
- Require out-of-fold CubeNet predictions/features for WaveLLM training rows in the predicted-mmWave-pose
  protocols.  Validation/test inputs are generated by the model fitted only on the corresponding training
  partition.  A cheaper teacher-forced or in-sample diagnostic may exist only with an explicit diagnostic label.
- Add a baseline `annotation_v1`/candidate `annotation_v2` comparison protocol.  The historical RTMW3D
  transform is preserved as an immutable baseline; an improved annotation can never overwrite it and needs
  stratified visual review plus objective failure/jitter/identity diagnostics before selection.
- Record a historical-replay boundary: old CSL-Daily `val.json` and `test.json` are byte-identical and can be
  used only to reproduce the former validation convention.  They are not an independent new holdout or a
  generalization result.

## Non-Goals

- Altering the in-progress `rebuild-csl-daily-simulation` implementation, its simulation equivalence fixture,
  or its data-delivery contract.
- Treating skeleton point-reflector simulation as a direct reproduction of the manuscript's MANO/ray-tracing
  description.
- Replacing the reviewer-required real-radar, participant-disjoint orientation/occlusion evaluation with
  CSL-Daily results.
- Loading, converting, or otherwise using the incoming historical WaveLLM bundle before its receipt/audit gate.
- Recreating legacy interfaces or importing legacy modules into `src/mmprism/`.

## Impact

- New canonical training/data modules, versioned configurations, fixtures, and integration tests will be added
  after this proposal is accepted.
- The Data Rebuild, OmniHand Training, WaveLLM Training, and Paper Revision workspaces receive a shared
  runbook but retain their respective production boundaries.
- Formal results will distinguish synthetic CSL-Daily control evidence, historical replay evidence, and
  reviewer-facing real-radar evidence.
